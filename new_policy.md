We collect information in a few different ways to provide and improve the Game.

We may collect information that you provide to us directly, such as when you:

*   Make an in-app purchase.

We and our partners automatically collect certain information when you play the Game. This information includes:

*   **Device and Usage Information:** We collect information about your device, including your device model, operating system, unique device identifiers (like your advertising ID), IP address, and your in-game activity (e.g., game progress, session time).
*   **Data Collected Through Our Partners:** We use third-party services that collect information to provide their services, for analytics, and for advertising. These partners include:
    *   **Kwalee:** As our publisher, Kwalee collects data for analytics and to improve the game. You can find their privacy policy here: [https://www.kwalee.com/privacy-policy/](https://www.kwalee.com/privacy-policy/)

You can learn more in Facebook's privacy policy: [https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/](https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/)

### 1.3. Data for In-App Purchases

When you make an in-app purchase (IAP), we collect information related to that purchase. This includes:

*   Your username (if you have one in the game).

This information is stored securely on a Virtual Private Server (VPS) and/or Google Cloud Functions to manage your purchases and provide you with the content you've bought.
